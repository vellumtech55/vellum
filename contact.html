Frequently Asked

What's the Vellum App Launcher? +

It's the easiest way to get Vellum products — download it once from the Products page, then use it to browse, install, and launch anything in the lineup.

Do I need to install anything before using a Vellum product? +

Yes — every Vellum product is built on the same Python runtime, so the Dependencies pack needs to be installed once before any other tool will run.

Are the Free Tools actually free? +

Yes. Everything on the Free Tools page runs directly in your browser — no account, download, or payment required.

What's the refund policy? +

Digital products are generally non-refundable once delivered, but requests are reviewed case-by-case. Please contact us before opening a chargeback — see the full terms in the EULA below.

A product isn't working for me — what do I do? +

Send a message using the form above with what you're seeing and which product it's for, and we'll help you troubleshoot.
